# Southeast Asia Backpacking Cheat Sheet 2026

Quick reference for budget travel in Thailand, Vietnam, and beyond. More at [golightly.fun](https://golightly.fun)

## Daily Budget Targets

| Country | Budget | Mid-range |
|---------|--------|-----------|
| Thailand | $20-30 | $40-60 |
| Vietnam | $15-25 | $30-50 |
| Cambodia | $15-25 | $30-45 |
| Laos | $15-20 | $25-40 |

## Must-Know Tips

- Always negotiate tuk-tuk prices before riding
- Street food is safe and delicious ($1-3/meal)
- SIM card at airport: $5-10 for 7 days unlimited
- Download Grab/Gojek for ride-hailing
- Book hostels on Agoda for best prices in Asia

## Top Routes

- **Chiang Mai Loop**: 3-5 days, $25/day
- **Bangkok to Islands**: 2 weeks, $30/day
- **Hanoi to Saigon**: 2 weeks, $20/day
