# Japan Budget Travel Guide 2026

Complete guide to traveling Japan on a budget. Visit [golightly.fun](https://golightly.fun) for full itineraries.

## Transportation Savings

- **JR Pass**: 7-day pass ~$200, covers bullet trains
- **Night Bus**: Tokyo-Osaka from $30 (Willer Express)
- **IC Cards**: Suica/Pasmo for local transport
- **Bicycle Rentals**: Many cities offer $3/day rentals

## Accommodation Under $30/night

- Capsule hotels from $20
- Hostels from $15 (dorm bed)
- Manga cafe overnight ~$10
- Temple lodging (shukubo) from $40

## Free Activities

- Meiji Shrine, Senso-ji, Fushimi Inari (free entry)
- TeamLab Borderless ($20 but worth it)
- Imperial Palace East Gardens (free)
- Tsukiji outer market (free to browse)

## Food Under $10

- Convenience store meals: $3-5
- Ramen/udon: $5-8
- Conveyor belt sushi: $2/plate
- Ekiben (station bento): $5-8
