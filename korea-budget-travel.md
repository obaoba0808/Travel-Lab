# Korea Travel on a Budget 2026

Seoul, Busan, Jeju - all affordable. Full guides at [golightly.fun](https://golightly.fun)

## Transport Hacks

- T-money card: 100 won discount per ride
- Korea Rail Pass: 2-5 day options from $50
- AREX airport train: $3.50 to Seoul Station
- Intercity buses: cheaper than KTX

## Free Things to Do

- Bukchon Hanok Village (free walking)
- N Seoul Tower base (free, elevator paid)
- Changdeokgung Secret Garden (seasonal free days)
- Hongdae street performances nightly
- Gamcheon Culture Village, Busan (free)

## Must-Eat Under $10

- Gimbap: $2-3
- Tteokbokki: $2-4
- Samgyeopsal: $8-12/person
- Army stew (budae jjigae): $6-8
- Bingsu: $5-8

## Jeju Island Tips

- Rent a car for $30/day (international license OK)
- Public buses cover most spots but slow
- 3 days minimum, 5 days ideal
- Seongsan Ilchulbong sunrise: free if you arrive early
