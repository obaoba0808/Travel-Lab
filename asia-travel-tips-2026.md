# Asia Budget Travel Tips 2026

Comprehensive budget travel guides for Japan, Korea, Taiwan, and Southeast Asia.

## Quick Tips

- **Japan**: Get a 7-day JR Pass for unlimited bullet train rides
- **Korea**: T-money card works on all Seoul public transport
- **Taiwan**: EasyCard covers MRT, buses, and even convenience stores
- **Thailand**: Songthaews are the cheapest way around Chiang Mai
